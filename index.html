About

Academic background and research perspective

My work is shaped by a dual interest in molecular biology and data-driven biological discovery.

I am a Research Officer at the Child Health Research Foundation (CHRF), with academic training in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST). My research centers on using genomics and computational methods to understand disease biology, especially in the context of infection, immune response, and cellular heterogeneity.

Across my projects, I have worked with single-cell RNA sequencing, bulk transcriptomics, metagenomics, statistical modeling, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in how computational analysis can uncover interpretable biological patterns from high-dimensional data while remaining grounded in experimental and clinical context.

My longer-term goal is to contribute to research that integrates wet-lab and computational approaches to address clinically meaningful questions in genomics, host-pathogen interaction, and molecular disease mechanisms.

Research Experience

Selected projects

These projects reflect a consistent focus on extracting biologically interpretable signals from sequencing data using rigorous analytical workflows.

Pediatric Single-Cell Atlas of the Nasopharynx

Developed and analyzed single-cell transcriptomic datasets to characterize epithelial and immune cell states in pediatric nasopharyngeal tissue, with emphasis on infection-associated remodeling and host-pathogen interactions.

scRNA-seq Seurat SingleR Cell annotation
Key outcome: Contributed to atlas-scale analysis of pediatric mucosal cellular diversity and infection-linked cell-state variation.

Mpox Transcriptomics Biomarker Study

Performed transcriptomic analysis and machine-learning-based biomarker discovery to identify candidate genes associated with Mpox infection, integrating differential expression results with classification modeling and biological interpretation.

DESeq2 Machine learning Biomarker discovery ROC/AUPRC
Key outcome: Helped identify candidate transcriptomic biomarkers and support a first-author publication in Scientific Reports.

Deep Learning for Cancer Latent Profiling

Applied variational autoencoders to transcriptomic data to learn latent representations of tumor biology and used attribution-based interpretation to identify genes associated with latent disease structure and downstream classification performance.

VAE Integrated Gradients Latent space analysis Python
Key outcome: Used deep representation learning to prioritize biologically meaningful latent drivers in cancer-related transcriptomic profiles.

Metagenomics for Meningitis Pathogen Detection

Worked on metagenomic sequencing analysis for pathogen detection in clinically complex meningitis cases, integrating microbial identification, genomic interpretation, and clinically relevant contextualization for improved understanding of suspected infections.

Metagenomics Pathogen detection Genome analysis Clinical genomics
Key outcome: Supported sequencing-based detection and interpretation of pathogens in cases where conventional diagnostic signals were limited.
